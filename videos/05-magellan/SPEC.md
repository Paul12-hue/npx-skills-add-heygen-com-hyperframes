# SPEC – Was wäre, wenn du 1519 mit Magellan um die Welt segelst?

GOAL:    Experience the first circumnavigation from the crew's point of view, from 270 men to 18.
         Twist: the logbook is a day behind (west-bound around the Earth).
OUTPUT:  HyperFrames, 1080 x 1920, 30 fps, 72 s. No voice. Background music: own arrangement on the
         Folia ground (Iberian, 16th c., public domain harmony), tools/music/folia.py → assets/bgm/folia.wav,
         −16 LUFS, peak −1 dBFS. Four passes of 8 bars (18 s each): departure / winter+strait / Pacific (sparse) / homecoming.
FACTS:   Departure from Sanlúcar de Barrameda 20 Sep 1519, 5 ships (Trinidad, San Antonio, Concepción, Victoria, Santiago),
         ~270 men (sources 237–270). Brazil Dec 1519. Wintering at San Julián (Patagonia) end of Mar–Aug 1520,
         mutiny in April 1520. Santiago lost in May 1520. Strait 21 Oct–28 Nov 1520 (38 days), San Antonio turns back.
         Pacific 98 days without landfall until Guam (6 Mar 1521); leather, sawdust, rats (Pigafetta).
         Magellan dies 27 Apr 1521 at Mactan (Philippines). Concepción burned (May 1521), Trinidad stays behind (Dec 1521).
         Victoria under Elcano back 6 Sep 1522 with 18 men. At Cape Verde the crew notices the log is one day behind.
LOOK:    Low-poly carracks (Burgundy cross on the mainsail), eight places: harbour at dawn, Atlantic, Patagonia snow,
         strait with glaciers, Pacific (day/night flicker), Philippines at sunset, storm on the way home, globe with route.
         Quick cuts through black (0.35 s) between places. Accent "Magellan" in #E2B65C.
HUD:     Date (German) · "Tag N" (from 20 Sep 1519) · ships / crew.
QA:      2, 7, 12, 19, 23, 27, 29, 36, 41, 47, 53, 56, 59, 63, 69 s.
