# SPEC – Was wäre, wenn der Mond immer näher kommt?

GOAL:    Watch the Moon grow over the North Sea until it breaks apart and Earth gets rings.
         Twist: in reality it moves away, 3.8 cm per year.
OUTPUT:  HyperFrames, 1080 x 1920, 30 fps, 72 s. No sound.
FACTS:   Distance 384,400 km. Apparent size ∝ 1/d, brightness ∝ 1/d², tidal force ∝ 1/d³
         (half the distance → 8x). North Sea tidal range ~3 m. Earth tides today ~30 cm.
         Full moon ~0.25 lux; at ~60,000 km ~40x → twilight (~10 lux), not daylight.
         Roche limit of the Moon (fluid body) ≈ 18,000 km. Debris forms a ring inside the Roche limit.
         The Moon recedes 3.8 cm/year; in ~600 million years no more total solar eclipses.
LOOK:    North Sea coast at night, low-poly: beach, dunes, red-and-white lighthouse with a rotating beam,
         houses with red roofs, jetty, boats. Moon disc with craters, moonglade on the water. Tide = sea level
         oscillating, amplitude ∝ tidal force (visually capped). Breakup: disc → fragments along the orbit.
         Space: Earth with a ring. Back to the calm coast at the end.
         Accent "Mond" in #8DB8E8.
